## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the final square app-icon artwork for Sliplet, an iOS-only personal receipt wallet. One single icon, 1024 by 1024 pixels, edge-to-edge opaque square canvas, no presentation board or phone mockup. Scene/background: very light warm ivory with a restrained soft illumination gradient, extending fully to every corner; do NOT pre-round the outer canvas. Subject: an original minimalist landscape cognac leather pocket wallet, front facing with very slight dimensional depth, a gently scooped open mouth, subtly raised stitched rim, rounded lower corners and soft side highlights. One clean ivory receipt slips upward from BETWEEN its back panel and front pocket, tilting slightly left, its TOP edge softly serrated like torn receipt paper; three short subtle charcoal printed bars and one shorter bar suggest receipt content, NO letters, digits, prices or logos. Keep the lower part of the paper visibly inside the pocket. Composition: bold centered wallet and receipt silhouette occupies roughly 75 percent of square width, receipt extends upward into the upper third; balanced generous margin for Apple's system icon mask. Distinctively receipt-first, not a payment card. Materials: elegant tactile leather, fine unobtrusive grain, restrained stitching that still reads when small; paper is matte with a soft lift and realistic contact shadow at the wallet mouth. Lighting: gentle upper-left studio light, believable soft cast shadow beneath wallet, distinct but delicate shadows separating receipt, wallet back and front. Style: premium contemporary iOS app artwork, minimalist but dimensional, clear sculpted shapes, warm neutral palette, restrained detail. Avoid excessive photorealism, clutter, glossy plastic, rainbow colors, currency symbols, text, checkmarks, metal clasps, watermarks, borders, outer rounded-square frame, inset icon tile. This is original Sliplet artwork inspired by the app's leather wallet and layered paper interaction, not a copy of any company's product or logo.

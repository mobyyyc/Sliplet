# P17-01 · Everyday usability baseline

Audited source revision `ccc9a28`, app version 0.3 (4), with Xcode 27.2 and iPhone 15 Pro Simulators on iOS 27.0. The normal Photos path used a fresh app store with AI disconnected and Apple Vision reading on device. The other journeys used isolated fictional stores. The audit used temporary UI probes and existing XCTest cases. It shipped no application changes.

## Observed journeys

| Journey | Actions observed | Outcome and timing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First and repeat Photos import | 9 taps, no scrolling gestures, 1 search entry in each of four runs. | First receipt and three subsequent receipts each finished without correction and reopened through item search. On-device reading came selected. The first read-to-review took 2.53 s. The three subsequent runs had a median of 2.59 s (2.44–2.59 s). Save-to-detail median on subsequent runs was 2.65 s (2.57–2.87 s). |
| Correct one amount on a 36-item receipt | Test with Cancel and retry: 8 taps, 2 scrolling gestures, 2 text entries. A separate manual full-form navigation probe needed 19 short drags to reach the last amount. | The required-field prompt jumped directly to the correct row in the conventional form. The source-backed editor was reached by expanding Suggested checks and opening its line. Cancel preserved the missing value and Apply enabled Finish after correction. The suggestion-to-source segment took 3.38 s. Finish-to-detail took 2.55 s. |
| Find a previous price | Search through history to the older receipt and back: 7 taps, no scrolling gestures, 1 search entry at the largest text size with Reduce Motion. | The matching row near the end of the older receipt was highlighted, and returning preserved the query and result position within 14 points. History-to-entry availability took 3.12 s. A compatible printed-price pair could save its comparison after verification. An each versus kilogram pair displayed an incompatibility message and offered no comparison to save. |
| Find an archived return receipt | 8 taps, no scrolling gestures, 1 search entry, including three taps to enable Include archived. | The exact item initially produced No matches. After including archived receipts, the same query found the archived 31-item receipt and opened its Original. Returning retained the query, archive filter, and result position within 12 points. Opening the result and then the original took 5.88 s in the driver. |
| Split between two people | 10 taps, no scrolling gestures or text entries, from a saved receipt with no split to a finalized split. | The two unnamed people could assign the purchases and choose Split equally for tax whose item scope was unclear. The completed amounts in this run were CAD 10.32 and CAD 5.31, totaling CAD 15.63. The split reopened with its totals visible. Finalize-to-totals took 2.81 s. |

Taps and gestures count the named task from its stated starting state. Text entry counts a sequence once, including search submission when applicable. Wallet seeding, archiving the fixture, and resetting between runs are excluded. All times include XCTest queries, taps, and idle waits. The repeated reading measure used three distinct fictional receipts of identical layout from the same merchant. These timings are a Simulator comparison baseline. Human correction time, device performance, and live AI latency still require separate measurements.

## Verification scope and reproduction

Eleven distinct UI methods passed across the staged runs and the scoped history rerun. The two existing large-text launch assertions remain recorded as failed. The custom history attempt that selected the underlying back button passed after scoping navigation to the visible receipt sheet. Light, dark, large text, Reduce Motion, and an opaque-controls split were exercised across the selected cases. VoiceOver operation on a phone, hardware camera, live AI, and frame pacing on a phone remain open. No phone installation took place.

- Photos uses four generated, high-contrast JPEGs with distinct search terms and matching subtotal, HST, and total. Seed them into a fresh Simulator Photos library, open the regular app with no launch arguments, and use the normal import menu and Search. The tap count ends when the item match opens the receipt.
- For correction use the existing `correction-long` preview. The generated original prints the final amount, and the draft omits that field. Exercise both the top required-field prompt and the Suggested checks route. `ReceiptCorrectionUITests` supplies the conventional-form and long-dark comparison checks.
- For history use the `purchase-history` preview with `--history-long-receipt` and `--p1004-price-fixture`. Start with the visible Search control. Open the older record with the matching item at the end. The incompatible variant supplies a printed per-kilogram price on the older receipts and per-item on the newer one. `PurchaseHistoryUITests` covers the compatible saved comparison.
- For archive seed one 31-item receipt with a late item unique in its collection and archive it before the measurement. Search its item with the default scope, enable Include archived, then open the result and Original and return.
- For split use the `split` preview with no named people and unclear tax markings. Assign the first item, select all unassigned items for the second person, split tax equally, finalize, close, and reopen. Use the existing dark and accessibility split variants for spot checks.
